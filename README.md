## 🔬 2. PHYSICAL ARCHITECTURE & GEOMETRY

The device is split into two primary components: the **Inverted Honeycomb Transceiver Bowl** and the **Modular Universal Resonator Puck**.

### 2.1 The Flat-Topped Cylinder Pupil Node
*   The center "Pupil" consists of a vertical, flat-topped cylindrical column cast directly into the main substrate body. 
*   This design provides a uniform, solid-to-solid contact plane for the 12-inch Universal Puck, eliminating flat-to-curved air gaps and stopping dielectric wave scattering. 
*   The vertical walls of the column act as a heavy-duty physical shear key, absorbing 100% of lateral rotational torque during high-speed spinning operations to protect the 3-6-9 pin index.

### 2.2 Graded Copper Honeycomb Passive Lens
The embedded copper honeycomb layer is no longer a uniform mesh. It features a precision-machined thickness gradient:
*   **Outer Rim (12–18 inch diameter):** Thickest profile (0.5 to 0.75 inches). Captures maximum field flux from the CW/CCW vein loops, acting as a high-capacity collector.
*   **Mid-Transition Zone:** Gradually tapering thickness profile. Smoothly compresses and directs the field lines inward with no sudden impedance spikes or standing-wave reflections.
*   **Inner Pupil Column:** Thinnest profile (0.1 to 0.2 inches). Focuses the accumulated energy into a tight, high-density collimated beam shooting up the flat top chimney face.

### 2.3 Dual-Sided Counter-Rotating Vector Veins
*   **Convex (Outside) Surface:** Features 6 primary crystalline quartz veins swept in an **18° Clockwise (CW) Spiral** to disperse outward signals for transmission.
*   **Concave (Inside) Surface:** Features matching crystalline quartz veins swept in an **18° Counter-Clockwise (CCW) Spiral** to vacuum incoming fields inward.
*   **The Continuous Lip:** The CW and CCW veins connect seamlessly at the outer rim via a continuous crystalline bend, forming an unbroken, closed loop.

## 🛠️ 5. LABORATORY MANUFACTURING SPECIFICATIONS

### 5.1 Single-Pour Vitrimer-B-Stage Process
To eliminate material cold joints and protect against delamination under high-frequency Burst Mode stresses, the bowl and pupil column are integrated into a single chemical matrix:
1. Machine or water-jet cut the copper honeycomb with the built-in edge-to-center thickness gradient.
2. Encapsulate the graded honeycomb in the quartz-epoxy substrate, resting exactly on the midpoint horizon.
3. During the Vitrimer-B-Stage semi-cure, the base bowl is brought to 70% cure before the final Pupil column resin is added, allowing the polymer chains to cross-link completely across the structural boundary.
4. Compress the unified mold to **15 MPa** to permanently pre-stress the matrix.
